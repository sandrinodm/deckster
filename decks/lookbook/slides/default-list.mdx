## Flock makes four decisions every 90 seconds

<Step at={1} focus>

- **Which drops go now:** the windows that close first

</Step>

<Step at={2} focus>

- **Which drops ride together:** neighbours, up to nine

</Step>

<Step at={3} focus>

- **In what order:** the route solver sets the stops

</Step>

<Step at={4} focus>

- **Which rider takes it:** the next one back at the hub

</Step>

<Notes>
`default` with a heading and four bullets, each in `<Step focus>`; use it when you talk through a list one point at a time and the room should look at the point you are on.
[click] First, Flock picks the drops whose windows close soonest. [click] Then it groups neighbours into a loop of up to nine. [click] The route solver orders the stops. [click] And the loop goes to the next rider back at the hub. All of it, every 90 seconds.
</Notes>
