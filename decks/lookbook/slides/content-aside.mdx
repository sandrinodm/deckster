We sell drops, but we work in loops.

- **Plan:** Flock builds loops of up to nine
- **Staff:** a rider's shift is six loops
- **Cost:** a fuller loop is a cheaper drop

<Aside>
### Loop

One rider's round from a hub and back: 9 drops, 68 minutes and 7.2 km at the median.
</Aside>

<Footnote>Median loop, October 2026. Source: Wrenhaul operating report, October 2026.</Footnote>

A merchant buys a drop, but a loop is what we actually run: one rider's round from the hub and back. Flock plans loops, we staff a shift as six of them, and because a rider is paid by the hour, a fuller loop is a cheaper drop.
