<Notes>
`chart` with a `<Scatter>`, a `trend` line, two highlighted points and a reference line; use it when two measures move together and a few points tell the story.
Each dot is one of our fourteen hubs: how many drops it makes per square kilometre against what a drop costs. The line is the trend: the denser the hub, the cheaper the drop. [click] At the two ends, Old Town makes 410 drops a km² at €1.98; Sandgate makes 120 at €3.12, the only hub that costs more than the €3.10 a drop earns. That is why we choose new cities by how many drops fit in a square kilometre.
