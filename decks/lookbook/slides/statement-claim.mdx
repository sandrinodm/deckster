A shop should promise *the same hour* without owning a van.

<Notes>
`statement` with `emphasis: underline`, on its default inverse surface; use it for the one claim the talk turns on, with the two or three words that carry it marked.
Say it plainly: the hour is what customers now expect, and a van is what most shops cannot afford or no longer drive into the centre. Everything after this is how we make that true.
</Notes>
