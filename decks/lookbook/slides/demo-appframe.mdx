`demo` with an `<AppFrame>` running a mini app from `apps/dispatch-sim`, its steps driven by the slide and a poster drawn from the same component for every step; use it when the product has to move in front of the room. It is scripted and needs no network.

[click] The route solver orders each loop's stops, and each route is about seventy minutes. [click] And each loop goes to a rider who is back at the hub: Aoife takes 17, Jonas takes 18.
