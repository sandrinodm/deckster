  do: Keep the promise
  dont: Avoid the shortcut
reveal: true
budget: 45s
do:
  - Batch waiting drops every 90 seconds
  - Keep a loop within 9 drops and 75 minutes
  - Send parcels over 25 kg to a partner van
  - Agree a new day when no window is possible
dont:
  - Keep the old five-minute batching cycle
  - Squeeze a tenth drop into a full loop
  - Ask a rider to carry a parcel over the limit
  - Move a delivery date without asking the recipient

Overflow is a planned handover, with the recipient kept informed.
