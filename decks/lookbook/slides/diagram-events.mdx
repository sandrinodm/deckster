<Notes>
`diagram` with a `<Flow>`, `<Packet>`s on the stream, a `<State>` that fails and recovers, and `<Focus>` on the retry; use it when the story is what happens when something breaks.
Every change a rider makes goes on the event stream once, and everything that cares reads it from there. [click] The rider taps delivered: Track updates, Insights counts it, and the merchant's webhook gets it, 0.4 seconds after the tap at the median. [click] Sometimes the merchant's server is down and answers 500. [click] We do not drop the event: the webhook is retried after two seconds, and again with longer gaps, until the merchant answers 200.
If asked: retries back off to two, four and eight seconds, then hourly for a day; the console's webhook log shows every attempt.
</Notes>
