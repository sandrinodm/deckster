A shop's checkout books a drop, and five of our systems handle it before a rider sees it. [click] The checkout calls Open Network with one POST. [click] The drop lands in the drops store and waits. [click] Every 90 seconds Flock takes the waiting drops and groups them into loops. [click] The route solver puts each loop's stops in order. [click] And the loop arrives on a rider's phone: nine stops, in order.
The event stream and the carrier dim while the drop travels: they matter for the next slides, not for this path.
