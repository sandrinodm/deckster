**Thesis.** Dense hubs make same-hour delivery cheaper. Lead Wrenhaul’s €14M round to open six hubs across four new cities.

### Why invest

- **Demand is growing.** Monthly drops tripled to 182,000 in October.
- **Full loops lower cost.** The median loop carries nine drops. Cost per drop fell from €3.40 to €2.34 in a year.

<Aside>
### Reconsider if

New hubs may take too long to fill, erasing the cost advantage.

Review the expansion if either of the first two hubs averages fewer than six drops per loop at week 12.
</Aside>

<Footnote>October 2026 operating report. Saltglass’s review threshold is illustrative.</Footnote>

<Notes>
Investment committee decision using `content` with an aside. Put the commitment to a vote: €8M of the €14M round. The demand and cost evidence justify testing the thesis in more cities. The strongest objection is that the density of the existing network may not carry over. Agree to revisit expansion if either of the first two new hubs averages fewer than six drops per loop at week twelve.
</Notes>
