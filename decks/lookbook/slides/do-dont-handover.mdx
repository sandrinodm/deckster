---
layout: do-dont
title: A handover ends with the recipient
eyebrow: At the door
budget: 30s
do:
  - Confirm who is taking the parcel
  - Tap delivered after the handover
dont:
  - Leave a parcel without permission
  - Mark a drop done from the street
---

<Notes>
`do-dont` with two examples per side and default headings; use it to teach a practical habit through concrete actions.
The last metre matters as much as the loop. A successful handover means someone agreed to receive the parcel, then the rider confirmed it in Rider.
</Notes>
