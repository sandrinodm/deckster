Overall survival at year 8 is 81 percent. The spread between neighbourhoods is the story: the best district keeps 94 percent of its trees, the worst 58.

- Wide pits beat tree grates every time
- Corner plots lose twice as many trees to vehicle strikes
- Watering in the first two summers matters more than species
