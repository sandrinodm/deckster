Year 8 survival by pit width:

- Under 1 m: 58 percent
- 1 to 2 m: 83 percent
- Over 2 m: 94 percent

<Aside>
The cost per surviving tree is lowest in the widest pits, because fewer replacements are needed.
</Aside>
