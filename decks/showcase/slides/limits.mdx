<Notes>
These are real limits, straight from the catalog an agent reads before it writes. A content slide takes a ninety-character title. A split, where the title shares the width with a picture, takes forty-four.

An author who knows the limit before writing does not write a title that needs shrinking. An agent that knows it does not either.

Transition: and when the content outgrows a form, you change the form.
</Notes>
