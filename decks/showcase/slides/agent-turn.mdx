<Notes>
Here is how little the agent may do. [click] You type in the panel; the host gets the prompt and which slide you are on. [click] It clones the deck's content, slides and assets only, into a working copy. [click] It runs one turn of the agent, [click] which edits the working copy and nothing else.

[click] A sync brings changes into the deck, and it takes content files only. A config, a stylesheet, a brand file: ignored and reported. [click] Then the host lints and tells you what changed.

Every turn has an undo.

Transition: this is what it looks like from the chair.
</Notes>
