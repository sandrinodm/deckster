A slide app lets you put anything anywhere, and that is exactly why brand and fit depend on discipline. Generated HTML is fast and unchecked: it looks right until a title gets longer.

Deckster gives up free placement on purpose. Look at the third row: that one it loses. In exchange the brand holds by construction and the fit is measured.
