A slide is a file. The brand owns every pixel. A linter tells the author, human or agent, what is wrong.

<Notes>
Three sentences, and the rest of the talk is evidence for them. [pause]

A slide is a file: you can diff it, review it, generate it. The brand owns every pixel: an author cannot pick a colour or a type size. And the linter renders the deck and measures it, so "it overflows" is a finding with a fix, not an opinion.
