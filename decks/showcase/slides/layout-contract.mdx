<Notes>
Four clauses. [click] Fields are typed, so `titel` with a typo is a build error. [click] Slots say what they take: this one a chart, that one three bullets. [click] The budget counts content, words and bullets, so a slide inside its budget in one brand is inside it in all of them. [click] And variants tighten the limits when you pick the dense version, four cards instead of three.
