<Notes>
The linter does not read your source and guess. [click] It serves the deck and opens every step of every slide in Chromium. [click] It measures what was drawn: every box, every line of text, the colours under the glyphs. [click] Forty-one rules run over that snapshot. Here one fires: overflow. The finding carries the file, the rule and the fix. [click] You fix it, run again, clean.

That is why the same check works for a person and for an agent: the finding is text with an instruction in it.
