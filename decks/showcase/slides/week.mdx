<Notes>
This deck was made this way. Two sessions with no slides at all: who is in the room, what should change for them, and the argument as nine sentences. Then slides in small batches, linted and looked at. Then review, then rehearsal.

The repository ships that process as a skill an agent can follow, which is why the first two sessions do not get skipped.
