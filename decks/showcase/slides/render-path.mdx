<Notes>
Follow the dot. [click] The file goes to the build, which checks it against the layout's contract: unknown field, wrong slot, title too long, and you get an error with the fix.

[click] The layout takes the validated content and fills its slots, reading every value from the brand.

[click] And the result lands on the canvas, scaled to whatever screen you have.

Two inputs the author never touches: the catalog and the brand.

Transition: those boxes are packages, and they stack.
</Notes>
