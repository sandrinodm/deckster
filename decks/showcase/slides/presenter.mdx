Press `p` in the deck and a second window opens for the speaker.

- The current and the next slide, with these notes
- A clock that scales every slide's budget to the talk
- Overview, go-to, laser and blackout, one key each
- `?` lists every shortcut

<Aside>

### deck present

One command opens the audience window on the projector and the presenter window on the laptop.

</Aside>

<Notes>
I am reading this from the presenter window. It shows this slide, the next one, the notes, and a clock.

The clock is the useful part: the deck knows how long the talk is and every slide has a budget, so it tells me whether I am ahead or behind, per slide.

deck present does the window placement for you: projector gets the slides, laptop gets the notes.

Transition: and after the talk, somebody asks for the file.
</Notes>
