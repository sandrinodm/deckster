aurora.webm, aurora-poster.jpg, aurora-part-1.jpg, aurora-part-2.jpg, aurora-part-3.jpg
  Made for the framework's components sample deck with ffmpeg's gradients source: ffmpeg -f lavfi -i
  "gradients=s=1600x900:d=9:r=30:c0=0x0c0e13:c1=0x2d4ae0:c2=0x34c3b0:c3=0xf5b53d:nb_colors=4:speed=0.012"
  -c:v libvpx-vp9 -crf 38 -b:v 0 aurora.webm. The poster is the frame at 4 s; aurora-part-1,
  -2 and -3.jpg are the frames at the end of each cued part (2.95 s, 5.95 s and 8.95 s).
  Nine seconds, 1600x900, no sound. No restrictions.
