title: A careful first summer keeps new trees alive
eyebrow: Watering wardens · 2027
surface: accent
labels:
  do: Help the roots establish
  dont: Avoid the common errors
reveal: true
budget: 45s
do:
  - Water slowly across the soil bed on a dry summer evening
  - Leave a clear ring around the trunk as you spread mulch
  - Check the soil below the surface before adding a bucket
  - Report damaged stakes and split ties to the arborist
dont:
  - Pour the whole bucket onto one spot beside the new trunk
  - Heap fresh mulch against the bark to hide the soil basin
  - Assume wet pavement means the soil below is damp enough
  - Tighten a loose tie until it rubs against the tree bark
---

Every watering warden follows the same guide in the first two summers after planting.

<Footnote>Larkfield watering guide, 2027; ask the duty arborist if a tree looks stressed.</Footnote>
