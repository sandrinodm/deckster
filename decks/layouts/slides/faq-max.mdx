questions:
  - q: Who waters trees through the first summers?
    a: Our crews water every new tree for three summers. A named arborist checks its growth each season.
  - q: What happens when a newly planted tree dies?
    a: We replace it in the next planting season. The replacement receives three summers of watering and care.
  - q: How do residents report damaged tree guards?
    a: Report the street and nearest house number to the tree team. A crew checks the guard on its next round.
  - q: Can a resident ask for a different species?
    a: Yes, before planting. The arborist checks soil, space and summer heat before choosing a species.
---

Every request reaches the crew for that street.

<Footnote>Larkfield Urban Forestry Division, 2027 planting programme. Care covers watering, guards and replacement trees.</Footnote>
