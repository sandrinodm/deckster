---
layout: do-dont
title: Water the roots, leave the trunk clear
budget: 30s
do:
  - Water slowly into the soil
  - Keep mulch away from the trunk
dont:
  - Spray the leaves at midday
  - Pile mulch against the bark
---

<Notes>
Two habits for Larkfield's watering wardens: wet the roots and protect the bark.
</Notes>
