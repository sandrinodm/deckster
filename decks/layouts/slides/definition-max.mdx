The share of a neighbourhood’s ground covered by tree crowns when seen from above. We measure streets, gardens and public land together so the survey captures shade across the whole area, regardless of ownership.

<Footnote>Larkfield Urban Forestry Division, annual canopy survey. All land inside the neighbourhood boundary is included.</Footnote>
