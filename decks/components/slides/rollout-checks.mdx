</Step>

<Step at={2} focus>

</Step>

<Step at={3} focus>
- **A rollback** runs green in staging the same day
</Step>

<Step at={4} effect="wipe">
A rollout that skips a check waits for the next window.
</Step>

<Notes>

A static shot or an export shows all three at full strength.
