<Step at={1}>
- **Eastgate:** 14 residents
</Step>

<Step at={2}>
- **City average:** 5 residents
</Step>

<Step at={3}>
- **Riverside:** 2 residents
</Step>

<Footnote>Heat-vulnerable: over 65, under 5, or living alone with a chronic condition. Sources: 2025 census and GP registers.</Footnote>
