title: Why the heat lands hardest in Eastgate
eyebrow: The problem
budget: 1m30s

- Canopy cover is 9%, the lowest of our fourteen districts
- 71% of the ground is roof, road or car park
- Two in three homes are flats with no cross-ventilation
- One resident in five is over 65 or under 5
- Night-time lows stayed above 24 °C for nine nights in July
- Fewer than half of households have air conditioning

<Footnote>Sources: 2026 aerial survey, 2025 census, Larkfield housing register.</Footnote>

<Notes>
These six facts compound. Hot ground, homes that cannot cool, and people who cannot cope with heat, all on the same twelve blocks.

The night-time figure is the one the hospital cares about most. Bodies recover at night; in Eastgate they cannot.
</Notes>
