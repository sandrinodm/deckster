A ten-year capital line, reviewed every three years against the canopy target.

- $4.2M a year from 2027 to 2036, $42M in total
- 6,000 large-species trees, 4,800 of them in Eastgate and Millbrook
- Three arborist posts to run planting and aftercare

<Aside>

### The target

Raise Eastgate's canopy cover from 9% to 25% by 2040, the level at which July afternoons stay below the hospital's heat-alert threshold.

</Aside>

<Notes>
The three-yearly review is the safeguard. If the pilot numbers do not hold, the committee can stop the line.

Millbrook is the second-hottest district; it gets the remaining trees from 2030.
</Notes>
