A mature maple shades 90 m² of pavement and evaporates up to 400 litres a day.

- Shaded asphalt runs up to 20 °C cooler
- The effect peaks after 15 to 20 years

<Notes>
This is the street from the cover. Ask the room to look at where the shadow falls: on the pavement and the front of the house, at three in the afternoon.

The 400 litres is a peak figure for a large tree on a dry day; the usual summer figure is about half.
