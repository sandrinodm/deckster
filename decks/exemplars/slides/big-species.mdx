Small ornamental trees are cheaper to plant, but most of the cooling comes from the largest crowns.

- A plane tree at 20 years shades eight times the ground of a flowering pear
- Large species need 12 m³ of soil, so we build connected pits under the pavement

<Notes>
Small species stay on streets under 9 m wide, where a large crown would reach the buildings.

This is the slide engineers will push back on. The soil volume is the reason big trees survive; without it they stall at ten years.

Each connected pit costs $2,900 more than a standard one and pays for itself by year 14, from avoided pavement repairs and cooling costs alone, not health savings.
</Notes>
