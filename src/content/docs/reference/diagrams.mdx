## Diagrams and parts

Seven components are diagrams: you put one on a slide. The other thirteen are parts, and each part's entry says where it goes ("Goes in"): a `<Node>` in a `<Flow>`, a `<Group>`, a `<Cycle>` or nested in its parent `<Node>` inside a `<Tree>`; a `<Hop>` only in a `<Journey>`; an `<Item>` only in a `<Layer>`. Below, each diagram is followed by its parts. A prop name a component does not take, or a value outside the ones it lists, fails the build naming the closest one.

`<Timeline>` here is the diagram; the `timeline` layout has [its own page](/reference/layouts/timeline/).

A node's `kind` sets its shape and icon (`service`, `database`, `queue` and so on, each with aliases), its `state` takes one of five values (`default`, `highlighted`, `muted`, `error`, `success`; words that mean the same are accepted), `<Edge>` takes options such as a dashed line, a route style or an arrow at both ends, and `card` picks the node style the brand defines. All four are listed under [node kinds, states, edges and cards](#node-kinds-states-edges-and-cards) at the end of this page.

`card` is a shared prop of `<Node>`, `<Actor>` and `<Item>`, and its own prop on `<Flow>`, `<SequenceDiagram>`, `<Layers>`, `<Layer>`, `<Cycle>` and `<Tree>`; `<Timeline>`, `<Milestone>` and `<Svg>` take none. The names a slide may use depend on the brand: `deckster brand <name>` lists them, and the five standard ones exist in every brand.

## Limits

`deckster lint` holds diagrams to these numbers, the same as `deckster diagrams --json` prints under `limits`:

- **Density.** More than 12 nodes or 16 edges shown at one step is a `diagram-too-dense` warning.
- **Grid.** 5 columns and 3 rows fit comfortably in the `diagram` layout. Past 7 columns or 4 rows the nodes are packed tighter than the gutters allow, and `diagram-too-dense` reports the diagram as cramped.
- **Text.** An edge label takes 18 characters on a line and wraps to 2 lines; past that it ends in an ellipsis, and the full text stays in the accessible summary. A node title wraps to 3 lines at word boundaries before the node grows taller than its row. A word broken across lines leaves at least 3 characters on each.
- **Hard limits.** Past these the build fails: 60 nodes, 120 edges, 24 particles in one packet or edge flow, 64 for a grid column, row, span or minimum size, and step 999 for an `at` or `until`.
