The `features` layout sets six to twelve capabilities in an open grid, each an icon from the brand's set with a short title and, for up to nine of them, a few words of detail. Use it for a product capability overview or a checklist of what a service includes.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
The `features` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. In 0.5.0, `deck lint` reports `layout: There is no "features" layout.`
</Aside>

The capabilities are `features:` in the frontmatter, each with an `icon` and a `title`. Six items take three columns and two rows, with no panels around them, so the grid reads as one inventory. This slide has no body or eyebrow, and `surface: inverse` sets it on the dark surface. The layout uses the largest type that fits the whole grid, so six short titles are set large.

Twelve items take four columns and three rows, in smaller type and without details. This slide adds an `eyebrow`, a body sentence and a `<Footnote>`, and sets `reveal: true`; the still shows the last step.

Nine items with a `detail` each take three columns, the icon beside the text.

## When to use it

- **The capabilities are peers.** Use [`cards`](/reference/layouts/cards/) for two to four ideas that each need a sentence, [`steps`](/reference/layouts/steps/) when they happen in order, and [`comparison`](/reference/layouts/comparison/) or [`table`](/reference/layouts/table/) when they are measured against alternatives. Five items fit neither: `features` needs six and `cards` stops at four.
- **Each item is an icon and a title.** The `title` takes up to 22 characters and the optional `detail` up to 28. From nine items on, titles take up to 14 characters, and from ten the details go.
- **Columns follow the count.** Six or nine items take three columns; seven, eight and ten to twelve take four.
- **The body is one sentence** of up to 14 words on what the items have in common; a source goes in `<Footnote>`, up to 16. The title takes up to 50 characters.
- **Reveal by row.** `reveal: true` brings in one row per step: two steps for six items, three for twelve. Put a `[click]` cue in the notes for each; see [builds](/write-slides/builds/).
- **Icons come from the brand's set.** [icons](/reference/icons/) lists the names all published brands share.

## Common mistakes

- **Details on ten or more items.** `deck lint` rejects the slide:

  ```console
  $ deck lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/capabilities.mdx
    error   schema (capabilities): features: Details are supported for at most 9 features.
            Fix: Remove details from every item, or split the inventory across slides.

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
  ```

- **Long titles in a dense grid.** A title that was fine with six items becomes a `schema` error from nine: "features[1].title: The features layout sets at most 14 characters here (three rows), got 16."
- **Too few items.** "features: Expected at least 6 features, got a list of 5 items."
- **An icon name the brand's set lacks.** "`features[1].icon: clockz-nope` is not an icon of the lucide set."

## Related layouts

- [`cards`](/reference/layouts/cards/): two to four ideas that each need a line of text.
- [`steps`](/reference/layouts/steps/): numbered along a line, because the order matters.
- [`comparison`](/reference/layouts/comparison/): alternatives measured on the same rows.
- [`table`](/reference/layouts/table/): exact values in rows and columns, such as a feature or price grid.
