Ask the audience which parcel to dispatch. A bakery bag fits on a cargo bike. A heavier flour box needs a van. Each choice takes the presenter through the checks that produce that decision.

## Author the slide

Write a `prompt` and two `branches` with distinct names. Give each branch a situation, two or three actions and an outcome. In the live slide, select a branch and follow its buttons to the outcome. Use **Back to choices** to try the other branch. The finished PDF and PowerPoint exports show both paths side by side.
