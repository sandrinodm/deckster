The `agenda` layout lists the parts of your talk as three to seven numbered rows, each with a title and, if you want them, a line of detail and a time. Show it after the cover to set out the plan, and again between parts to show the room where they are.

With five to seven items, the detail moves beside the title on one line and the titles are set a step smaller. A day's programme with clock times fits this form.

## When to use it

- **For the plan of the talk.** Each item is a part you will present. Points of an argument belong on a [`content`](/reference/layouts/content/) slide, and a process on [`steps`](/reference/layouts/steps/).
- **Three to seven parts.** The schema takes no fewer and no more. More than seven parts need grouping first.
- **Again between parts.** Repeat the same items and set `current:` to the part that starts (1 is the first). Leave `current:` out on the first agenda.
- **Short labels.** `title` takes up to 40 characters, `detail` up to 44 and `time` up to 7 (`10 min`, `09:30`). With five to seven items the limits drop to 36 and 32.
- **A practical note** (breaks, where the slides are) goes in `<Footnote>`, up to 18 words. The slide title is one line of up to 48 characters.

## Common mistakes

- **Long titles in a longer agenda.** A title that fits a four-item agenda can be too long once you add a fifth item, because the "5 to 7 items" variant tightens the limits. `deck lint` reports it as a `schema` error that names the item:

  ```console
  $ deck lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/day-plan.mdx
    error   schema (day-plan): items[0].title: The agenda layout sets at most 36 characters here (5 to 7 items), got 39.
            Fix: Shorten it to the essentials; `deck layouts --json` lists the limits of each variant.

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
  ```

- **A `current:` past the last item.** On an agenda of five items, `current: 6` is a `schema` error: "current: 6 names item 6, but there are 5 items."
- **Text outside `<Footnote>`.** The layout draws only its items and the footnote. A paragraph under the frontmatter is not shown, and `deck lint` does not report it. Put what you will say in `<Notes>`.
- **Items that change between copies.** The repeated agenda only says "we are here" when its items match the first one. Copy the slide and change only `current:` and the title.
