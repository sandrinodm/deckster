Use the `title` layout for the cover of your deck: the deck title set large on the bottom line, a one-line subtitle, and who is presenting and when. You can add a photo beside the text or behind it, or leave it out for a type-only cover.

Notice `picture: full`: the photo in `<Media>` covers the whole slide, and the logo and the text block each sit on a dark scrim of their own, in light text whatever the surface. The catalog advises a photo that is calm in its lower left (sky, wall, road), since that is where the text block sits.

Without a photo, `align: center` centres the logo, title and subtitle for a keynote cover:

## When to use it

- It is the first slide. The catalog's guidance is to use it once, first; use [`section`](/reference/layouts/section/) to open each part of the talk.
- The deck title is short. `title:` is required and takes at most 36 characters, three lines of display type beside a cover photo.
- The subtitle is one sentence of at most 14 words, in the body. `byline:` (up to 40 characters) and `date:` (up to 24) sit on the bottom row.
- Choose how the picture sits:
  - No `<Media>`: a type-only cover on the gradient surface, the layout's default.
  - `<Media>` alone (`picture: side`, the default): the photo fills the right five columns to the slide's edges.
  - `picture: full`: the photo runs across the whole slide, for a picture that is calm in its lower left (sky, wall, road).
  - `align: center`: only on a type-only cover; with a photo, the text stays on the left.

The cover hides the page chrome by default (`chrome: false`). The logo sits at the top left by default and moves to the top centre on a type-only cover with `align: center`.

## Common mistakes

- **A long title.** A 47-character title fails [`deck lint`](/review/lint/) with a `schema` error: "title: The title layout sets at most 36 characters, got 47." Put the rest in the subtitle.
- **A long subtitle.** A 19-word subtitle fails with `content-budget`: "The body slot holds 19 words; its layout allows 14."
- **More than one picture.** `<Media>` takes one image with alt text. Two images fail with `slot-content`: "The media slot holds 2 images; the title layout takes exactly one: image there."
- **A busy photo behind the text.** With `picture: full`, the text covers the lower left, so a subject there ends up under the title. Use the side picture instead and set `focal` on the `<Image>`, as the lookbook's `cover` slide does.
- **A cover for every part.** The catalog keeps `title` for the first slide: a part opens with `section`, and the talk ends on [`closing`](/reference/layouts/closing/).
