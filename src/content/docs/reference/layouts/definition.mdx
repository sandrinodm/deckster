The `definition` layout sets one term as a dictionary entry under a title that states the point: the term in the display style, an optional pronunciation and category on a small line, its meaning in one or two sentences, and an optional example. Use it to settle what a specialist word, a product concept or a measure means before the argument depends on it.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
The `definition` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. In 0.5.0, `deckster lint` reports `layout: There is no "definition" layout.`
</Aside>

## When to use it

- **One term.** The `term:` is up to 20 characters. For a glossary of three or more terms side by side use [`cards`](/reference/layouts/cards/) or [`table`](/reference/layouts/table/).
- **The meaning is one or two sentences.** The body is required, text only, up to 36 words. A longer explanation belongs on [`content`](/reference/layouts/content/).
- **Each extra is optional.** `pronunciation` takes up to 32 characters, `kind` (a part of speech such as `noun`, or a category such as `Service promise`) up to 24, and `example`, one sentence, up to 100. `labels.example` takes up to 16 characters, so translate it when the deck is not in English.
- **Write a pronunciation as a respelling.** IPA characters that the brand's text font lacks are drawn from a substitute font in PowerPoint, and a respelling such as `LOOP` or `KAN-uh-pee` is the safe choice.
- **The title states the point,** up to 50 characters, and the `<Footnote>`, up to 16 words, gives the source or the scope of this definition.
- **The entry shows whole.** There is no `reveal`. A short entry is set larger, and a longer one steps down to fit.

## Common mistakes

- **Bullets in the body.** The meaning is text, and `deckster lint` reports a list with the `slot-content` rule:

  ```console
  $ deckster lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/definition-list.mdx
    error   slot-content (definition-list): The body slot holds a list of 2 items; the definition layout sets it as text.
            Fix: Write it as a sentence, or move the points to a layout made for a list (content or split; `deckster layouts`).

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings in 1 slide (1 step checked).
  ```

- **A paragraph for a meaning.** Past 36 words, `content-budget` reports "The body slot holds 46 words; its layout allows 36." Cut it to the meaning and say the rest.
- **A phrase for a term.** `term: Urban canopy cover across the district` is a `schema` error: "Expected a term of at most 20 characters, got "Urban canopy cover acro…" (38 characters)." Name the word and put the qualifier in the body.
