The `image-reveal` layout opens on a title, then reveals a picture on the next press. On step 0 the slide reads as a title slide. On step 1 the title and subtitle move into a full-width bar at the bottom or top of the slide, and the picture fills the rest. Use it to set up a picture, screenshot or diagram with one line before the room sees it.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.10.0">
The `image-reveal` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.10.0 or later. In 0.9.0, `deckster lint` reports `layout: There is no "image-reveal" layout.`
</Aside>

## How the two steps work

The layout always has two steps. Nothing in the slide turns them on, and there is no option to turn them off.

- **Step 0, the title beat.** The `eyebrow`, title and subtitle are set as on a title slide, over the slide's surface (`gradient` unless the frontmatter sets `surface`) and any `background:`. The picture is hidden.
- **Step 1, the reveal.** The title and subtitle move into a full-width bar docked to the bottom (`dock: bottom`, the default) or the top (`dock: top`). The bar has its own surface and a border on the edge that faces the picture. The picture fills the rest of the slide.

The move between them is animated with the brand's slow duration and emphasized easing, and runs backwards when you go back. Viewers who ask for reduced motion see the slide switch without the animation. Static mode, `deckster shot` and exports show the last step, the docked bar and the picture. `deckster shot <deck> <slide> --steps` writes one picture per step. See [builds](/write-slides/builds/) for how steps count.

## When to use it

- **A picture that needs a beat first.** Name the subject, let the room read it, then reveal it. When the picture should be seen at once, use [`image`](/reference/layouts/image/). For the deck's cover use [`title`](/reference/layouts/title/), and to open a part of the talk use [`section`](/reference/layouts/section/).
- **Keep the text short.** `title:` is required and takes up to 44 characters. The subtitle is the body: one sentence of up to 16 words, text only. It is optional. `eyebrow:` takes up to 40 characters and shows above the title.
- **Put one picture in `<Media>`.** It is required: an image file with alt text, as a Markdown image or `<img>`. [Media](/write-slides/media/) explains how `src` paths resolve.
- **Choose `cover` or `contain`.** `fit: cover` (the default) fills the space beside the bar edge to edge, cropped around `focal`, which defaults to `center`. Set `focal` (`top`, `center 30%`, `40% 20%`) to keep the subject in view. `fit: contain` shows the whole picture uncropped with space around it, which suits a screenshot.
- **Treatment follows `fit`.** Without `treatment`, `contain` shows the picture plain and `cover` uses the brand's default treatment. Set `treatment: plain` to keep a photo in its own colours. [Fonts and assets](/brands/fonts-and-assets/) lists the treatments.
- **Style the bar with a surface.** `barSurface` takes `default` (the default), `inverse`, `accent` or `gradient`, each a surface every brand has. The bar's text takes that surface's colours.
- **Override the bar's colours only when the surfaces don't fit.** `barBackground` takes a CSS colour or gradient and `barBorder` a CSS colour, each up to 120 characters, such as `#111827` or `transparent`. Neither may contain `url()`, `expression()`, a semicolon or a brace. The text keeps the colours of `barSurface`, so pair a dark `barBackground` with `barSurface: inverse`.
- **Page chrome is off.** `chrome` defaults to `false`, which hides the page number, logo and progress bar. Write `chrome: true` to show them.

## Common mistakes

- **A subtitle longer than one line.** The body takes 16 words:

  ```console
  $ deckster lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/hub.mdx
    error   content-budget (hub): The body slot holds 20 words; its layout allows 16.
            Fix: Cut it to the essentials, split the slide, move detail into speaker notes, or pick a layout made for more content (`deckster layouts`).

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings in 2 slides (3 steps checked).
  ```

- **The picture outside `<Media>`.** A Markdown image at the top level of the slide is not the media slot, and `deckster lint` reports a `schema` error: "The image-reveal layout needs its media slot."
- **A title over 44 characters.** It is a `schema` error: `title: The image-reveal layout sets at most 44 characters, got 61.`
- **A bar background that loads a file.** `barBackground: "url(../assets/hub-square.svg)"` is a `schema` error: `barBackground: Expected a bar background as a CSS color or gradient without url(), semicolons or braces`. Put pictures in `<Media>`.
- **Linting the drawing that `deckster new slide` writes with 0.10.0.** `deckster new slide <deck> <id> --layout image-reveal` writes the catalog's example, whose `<Media>` holds a drawn placeholder that `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` crops in the area beside the bar. `@deckster/cli` 0.10.0 measured the cropped shapes in full and reported two `canvas-overflow` errors on step 1, such as "extends past the canvas: 87px past the top edge". 0.10.1 measures a shape by what its `<svg>` shows, so the example lints clean; upgrade, or replace the drawing with your picture.
