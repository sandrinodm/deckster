Wrenhaul invests in a reliable delivery window and accepts a smaller service area. Plot the competing offers against the same factors so the audience can see the cost of that choice.

## Author the slide

Name four to six `factors` so a higher score has a clear meaning. For example, a higher score on **Lower price** represents a lower price. Score the two `alternatives` and your `offer` from 1 to 5, in factor order and using the same scale. Explain the scoring basis in the notes. Use `emphasize` and `tradeoff` to name the priority and what you give up. With `reveal: true`, the slide opens on the alternatives, then adds your offer and finally the tradeoff.
