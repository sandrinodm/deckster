The `split` layout sets text beside a picture or the evidence for it: a title and a short paragraph or bullets on one side, and a photo, chart, diagram, table, video or component on the other. Use it when the point needs something to look at and a few sentences to explain it.

`ratio: 7:5` gives the text seven of the twelve columns, `side: left` puts the picture on the left, and `bleed: true` runs it to the edges of the slide. With a bleed, the caption moves to the foot of the text column.

<SlideShot deck="lookbook" slide="split-donut" alt="A split slide titled Two kinds of shop pay 59%, with a short paragraph on the left and, on the right, a donut chart of revenue by merchant type with two slices in blue and 59% in its centre." />

Anything but a photo is centred in its frame, not cropped: here a `<Donut>` at `ratio: 5:7`, which gives the chart the wider side.

## When to use it

- **There is something to show.** With nothing to show, use [`content`](/reference/layouts/content/). When the photo needs only one line of text, use [`image`](/reference/layouts/image/).
- **The text explains the media.** A chart that stands alone with a one-line "so what" belongs on [`chart`](/reference/layouts/chart/); split is for a chart that needs a paragraph beside it.
- **Pick the ratio by what matters more.** `ratio:` shares the columns text:media, `1:1` (the default), `5:7` or `7:5`, and `side:` puts the media `left` or `right` (the default).
- **Fit the text to the ratio.** The narrower the text column, the less it holds: `5:7` takes the fewest words, three bullets and a shorter title, and `bleed: true` takes a few words more off. The reference below gives each limit.
- **Credit the media** in `<Caption>`. A source for the text goes in `<Footnote>`.

## Common mistakes

- **Bullets that fit at `1:1` but not at `5:7`.** Narrowing the text column tightens its budget:

  ```console
  $ deck lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/shade.mdx
    error   content-budget (shade): The body slot holds 4 bullets; its layout allows 3 (ratio 5:7).
            Fix: Cut it to the essentials, split the slide, move detail into speaker notes, or pick a layout made for more content (`deck layouts`).

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings in 1 slide (1 step checked).
  ```

- **Leaving out `ratio:` and writing for the widest column.** The default is `1:1`, so a 44-word body is over budget: "The body slot holds 44 words; its layout allows 40 (ratio 1:1)."
- **The media outside `<Media>`.** The slot is required; without it `deck lint` reports a `schema` error, "The split layout needs its media slot."
- **A chart or diagram that sits small.** Only photos fill the frame. Give other media `height: 100%` to fill it.

## Related layouts

- [`content`](/reference/layouts/content/): the same text with nothing beside it.
- [`image`](/reference/layouts/image/): one photo across the whole slide with at most a line of text.
- [`chart`](/reference/layouts/chart/): one chart filling the slide, with a short "so what" in `<Aside>`.
- [`screen`](/reference/layouts/screen/): a product screen shown whole in a drawn frame, when the picture matters more than the text.
