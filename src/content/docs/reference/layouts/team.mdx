Use the `team` layout to show the people behind the work, two to eight of them, each with a name, a role and a photo. You list them in the frontmatter, and the layout picks the arrangement from how many there are; for two to four founders it also sets why each is the right person and where they worked before.

## When to use it

- **Two to eight people.** `members` is required, each with a `name` and a `role`, and optionally a `photo` and a `focal` point that keeps the face in view when the photo is cropped.
- **The count sets the arrangement.** Three or four stand in one row; five or six sit in a grid of three; seven or eight are a roster in two columns. Text gets shorter as the grid gets denser; the reference below gives the limits.
- **The audience is judging the founders.** On a slide of two to four people, each may carry an `edge` (a line on why they are the right person) and `previously:`, up to three past organisations, each a name and a logo file.

For one speaker, use [`bio`](/reference/layouts/bio/); for a larger group, a [`table`](/reference/layouts/table/).

## Common mistakes

**An edge on a larger team.** Only a slide of two to four people has room for `edge` and `previously:`. Add a fifth person and the build fails; [`deck lint`](/review/lint/) reports it before it renders any slide:

```console
$ deck lint launch-review
decks/launch-review/slides/team.mdx
  error   schema (team): members[0].edge: A team of 5 has no room for `edge`; only a slide of two to 4 people shows it.
          Fix: Remove `edge` from the members, or show the founders or leads on a slide of their own, two to 4 of them.

launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
```

**A role that fit in a row but not in a grid.** Going from four people to five tightens the role to 30 characters: `members[0].role: The team layout sets at most 30 characters here (grid of 5 or 6), got 44.` Rewrite the roles when you add someone.

**The names in the body.** Name the people in `members:`; the body is one sentence on what they do together. At 15 words, `content-budget` reports `The body slot holds 15 words; its layout allows 14.`

## Related layouts

- [`bio`](/reference/layouts/bio/): one person, usually the speaker, with a large portrait and a few facts.
- [`logos`](/reference/layouts/logos/): organisations rather than people.
- [`quote`](/reference/layouts/quote/): one person speaking for the point, with their portrait.
- [`table`](/reference/layouts/table/): a group larger than eight.

Where a `photo` path points is on [media](/write-slides/media/).
