A customer can see a new arrival time without seeing the dispatch team change a rider’s route. The blueprint puts those two actions in the same column and names the team accountable for the handoff.

## Author the slide

Write two to four `stages` in journey order. For each stage, fill in `customer`, `frontstage`, `backstage` and `owner`. Point `handoff.stage` at the column where the responsibility moves, counting from one. With `reveal: true`, the first advance shows backstage work and the second adds owners and highlights the handoff.
