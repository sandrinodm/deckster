Show what a proposed service would feel like to its customer. The bakery ties a bag, the rider carries it across the canal and the recipient takes it at the door. Following the same bag makes the promise concrete.

## Author the slide

Give each of the three `scenes` a moment, an action, a detail and an image. Pick a consistent subject across the images and describe what changes in the captions. With `reveal: true`, the first scene opens the story and the next two advances complete it.
