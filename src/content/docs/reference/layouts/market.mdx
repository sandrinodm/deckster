The `market` layout sets two or three nested sizes of a market, outermost first, each with a label, a figure and a one-line note. Circles drawn with areas that follow the figures sit beside a list that gives every size the same stack. Use it to show total, serviceable and obtainable demand, or a whole market and the segment you can reach.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
The `market` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. In 0.5.0, `deckster lint` reports `layout: There is no "market" layout.`
</Aside>

## When to use it

- **The sizes are nested.** Each one is a part of the one before. For independent figures use [`stats`](/reference/layouts/stats/), for arithmetic the audience should check use [`equation`](/reference/layouts/equation/), and for a longer series use [`chart`](/reference/layouts/chart/).
- **Two or three sizes.** Each has a `label` of up to 20 characters, a `value` of up to 8 characters including its currency and unit (`€3.0B`, `$640k`, `7,000`) and a `note` of up to 40.
- **The figures are exact and in one unit.** Write a number, or a figure with a currency prefix, a `k`, `M`, `B` or `T` suffix and commas for thousands. Suffixes may differ between sizes; currencies may not. A figure the layout cannot read as an exact amount is still valid, but the slide then sets the list without circles. So does a pair of sizes too close for two circles with room for labels.
- **A very small part stays visible.** A circle under about 0.4% of the whole by area is drawn at a minimum size and labelled "Marker enlarged". `labels.minimum` and `labels.share` (default `{share} of {parent}`) change that wording.
- **One sentence of context.** The body, up to 14 words, defines the market or its unit. The `<Footnote>` takes the source and date, up to 16 words.
- **Name the sizes one at a time** with `reveal: true`, and put a `[click]` cue in the notes for each. See [builds](/write-slides/builds/).

## Common mistakes

- **Smallest size first.** A figure larger than the one before it is a `schema` error:

  ```console
  $ deckster lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/market-order.mdx
    error   schema (market-order): sizes.1.value: A nested market cannot be larger than its parent.
            Fix: List sizes from the whole market to the smallest part within it.

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
  ```

- **Two currencies.** `$4.2M` followed by `€640k` is a `schema` error: "sizes.1.value: Nested market figures must use the same currency or unit." The fix adds that compact magnitudes may differ.
- **A fourth size.** The schema stops at three: "Expected at most 3 market sizes". Show the extra scope on another slide.
