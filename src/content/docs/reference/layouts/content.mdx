Use the `content` layout to explain one idea in words: a title that states the point, then a short paragraph, a few bullets, or a sentence followed by bullets. Add a definition or key fact beside the text, or widen the body for a table, chart or component.

Notice the `<Aside>`: a `###` heading and one sentence, set in a column of its own beside the body. Filling it lowers the body's budget from 72 words to 56, which is why the body here is one sentence and three short bullets.

With `width: full`, the body runs across all twelve columns instead of the eight-column reading measure, so a component such as `<StatRow>` gets the whole width.

## When to use it

- **One idea, said in words.** The body is required and holds up to 72 words and six bullets. The title states the takeaway in up to 90 characters.
- **A term the point depends on.** Put it in `<Aside>`: up to 36 words, text or a number, never a list.
- **A source.** `<Footnote>` takes up to 30 words on the bottom line.
- **A table, chart or component under a sentence.** Write `width: full`. The body slot accepts text, bullets, a table, a chart or a component.

Short text is set larger and placed in the middle of the space, so a sparse slide does not look empty. When the point needs a picture beside it, use [`split`](/reference/layouts/split/); when it rests on numbers, use [`stats`](/reference/layouts/stats/).

## Common mistakes

- **Too many bullets.** A seventh bullet is a [`content-budget` error](/reference/lint-rules/): "The body slot holds 7 bullets; its layout allows 6." Merge two, or split the slide.
- **Forgetting that the aside shrinks the body.** A 61-word paragraph passes alone but fails once `<Aside>` is filled: "The body slot holds 61 words; its layout allows 56 (with aside)."
- **A list in the aside.** The aside takes text or a number. Two bullets there are a `slot-content` error: "The aside slot holds a list of 2 items; the content layout sets it as text or number."
- **An empty body.** A slide with only a title and `<Notes>` fails with a `schema` error: "The content layout needs content outside its slot tags."
- **A wide component at the reading width.** A chart or table in the default `width: text` gets eight columns. Set `width: full` for it.

## Related layouts

- [`split`](/reference/layouts/split/): text beside a photo, chart, diagram or table, when there is something to show.
- [`default`](/reference/layouts/default/): one centred column for a heading and a lead or a short list, when no specific layout fits.
- [`stats`](/reference/layouts/stats/): two to four big numbers, when the point is their size.
- [`cards`](/reference/layouts/cards/): two to four equal ideas, each on a card, instead of a list.

How the body text is sized and stepped down is on [text and fitting](/write-slides/text-and-fitting/).
