The dispatch queue makes one promise to the shop. Pull its layers apart to show the delivery window, the route that can meet it and the rider available to take it.

## Author the slide

Supply two or three aligned images in `layers`, with the product face first. Keep them the same size and use a wide aspect ratio, ideally about 4:1. Give each image a label, detail and alternative text. With `reveal: true`, the slide opens assembled and each advance exposes another layer. Use `takeaway` to state what the layers make possible together.
