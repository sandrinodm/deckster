The `exercise` layout sets a task for the audience: a title naming what to do, the duration as a large figure, two to five numbered instructions and a one-line output naming what to produce, with an optional line saying who works together. Use it for a workshop activity, a short practice task or a discussion that must end with something concrete, and leave it on screen while people work.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
The `exercise` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. In 0.5.0, `deckster lint` reports `layout: There is no "exercise" layout.`
</Aside>

## When to use it

- **The audience does the task.** The slide is a brief to work from. To explain a process the audience watches, use [`steps`](/reference/layouts/steps/), and for the parts of the session use [`agenda`](/reference/layouts/agenda/).
- **A timebox, a list and a deliverable.** `duration`, `instructions` and `output` are required. The duration is up to 8 characters with its unit, such as `10 min`.
- **Two to five instructions.** Each is up to 80 characters, or 64 with four or five, and starts with a verb.
- **Name what people produce.** `output` is one line of up to 60 characters, and `groups`, up to 24, says who works together, such as `In pairs`.
- **Context is optional.** The body takes one sentence, up to 14 words, and the `<Footnote>` up to 16.
- **The labels are fixed.** "Timebox", "Output" and "Groups" are written by the layout in English, in the brand's label style, and there is no `labels` field to change them.
- **It shows whole.** There is no `reveal`; the brief stays visible throughout.
- **Speaking time and working time differ.** The slide's `budget` is how long you plan to speak, and `duration` is the audience's time. See [MDX slides](/write-slides/mdx-slides/).

## Common mistakes

- **Long instructions in a long list.** The limit drops from 80 to 64 characters at four instructions, so a line that was fine in a list of three becomes a `schema` error:

  ```console
  $ deckster lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/exercise-long.mdx
    error   schema (exercise-long): instructions[0]: The exercise layout sets at most 64 characters here (four or five instructions), got 71.
            Fix: Shorten it to the essentials; `deckster layouts --json` lists the limits of each variant.

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
  ```

- **Asking for `reveal: true`.** The exercise has no such field. `deckster lint` reports a `schema` error: ``"reveal" is not a frontmatter field.`` and lists the layouts that have it.
- **The task in the body.** The body is context, up to 14 words. Write each action as an item of `instructions:`.
- **A single instruction.** The schema asks for at least two: "Expected at least 2 instructions".
