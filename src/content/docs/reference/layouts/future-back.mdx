Start on the right with a shop that can offer the hour at checkout. Move left to the capabilities that promise requires, then to a four-week pilot that can test them.

## Author the slide

Put the date, observable outcome and image in `future`. Name two `capabilities` and why the outcome depends on each. Finish with one `bet` containing a time window, an action and a test. Use `reveal: true` to uncover the chain in that order.
