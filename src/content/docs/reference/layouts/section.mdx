Use the `section` layout for the divider that opens each part of your talk: a part number, a title of two to five words and an optional one-line summary. Give every part one and the room always knows where the talk has got to.

Notice the number: with `number: 3` and `numeral: large`, it is set as 03 in the surface's own tone, behind the title and running off the bottom edge. Every part opener in the [lookbook](/examples/lookbook/) writes the same fields, so the dividers read as a set.

Fill `<Media>` with one image and the divider changes shape: the picture fills the right five columns edge to edge and the text keeps to the left.

## When to use it

- **One per part, in order.** The catalog asks for every divider on the same surface and with the same options. Nothing in [`deck lint`](/review/lint/) checks that, so pick the fields once and copy them.
- **A short title and at most one sentence.** `title:` is required and takes 40 characters. The summary is plain text of up to 16 words, or 14 when `<Media>` is filled. When you leave it out, its two lines stay empty, so every divider sets its title at the same height.
- **The look is set by three fields.** `numeral: large` makes the number a landmark. Leave it out and the number sits small on the top line. `align: center` centres the number, title and summary, but has no effect beside a picture. The divider defaults to the `accent` surface and hides the page number, logo and progress bar (`chrome: false`).

Pick another layout when the slide is not a divider. The cover of the deck is [`title`](/reference/layouts/title/). A list of every part with the current one marked is [`agenda`](/reference/layouts/agenda/), and a single claim is [`statement`](/reference/layouts/statement/).

## Common mistakes

- **A large numeral with no number.** `numeral: large` without `number:` fails with a `schema` error at `numeral`: "`numeral: large` sets the part number large, but the slide has no `number:`." Add `number: 2`, or remove `numeral`.
- **A list as the summary.** The body slot takes text only. Three bullets under the title get a `slot-content` error: "The body slot holds a list of 3 items; the section layout sets it as text." Write one sentence, and save the points for the part's first slide.
- **A number that is too long.** `number:` is a whole number from 0 to 99 (shown with two digits, so `3` reads 03) or text of up to three characters (`IV`, `Q3`). Anything longer is a `schema` error.
- **Dividers that do not match.** One divider with `numeral: small` or another surface among large-numeral accent ones breaks the set. Lint does not catch it.

## Related layouts

- [`title`](/reference/layouts/title/): the cover, used once, first, rather than a part opener.
- [`agenda`](/reference/layouts/agenda/): shows all the parts at once, with `current:` marking the one that starts.
- [`statement`](/reference/layouts/statement/): one sentence set large, a claim and not a heading.

The lookbook gives every part opener `transition: cover`; see [transitions and morphs](/write-slides/transitions-and-morphs/).
