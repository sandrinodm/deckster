Use the `gallery` layout to show two to four pictures together: screens of a product or a flow, before and after photos, or one subject seen from several places. The first picture is the lead and takes the largest frame; the others share the rest, each with an optional caption under it.

Notice three things in the source. The doorstep photo comes first, so it is the lead on the left. Each picture has a `focal:` that keeps its subject in view when the frame crops it. And `surface: default` sets the slide on the brand's default surface instead of the layout's own, `inverse`.

## When to use it

- You have two to four pictures and one point about them. `pictures:` is required and takes two to four entries; the title (up to 60 characters) says what they show together.
- One picture matters most. Put it first: with two pictures the lead takes the left two thirds and the other a tall frame, as a phone screen needs; with three it takes the left seven columns; with four, the left half.
- The words are short. The body is one sentence of at most 14 words, and a caption is at most 64 characters, or 40 when there are four pictures.
- Screenshots should not be cropped. Set `fit: contain` on each one to show it whole, as the lookbook's `gallery-screens` slide does.

Pick another layout when the pictures are not the whole slide:

- One picture with text beside it: use `split`.
- One photo across the slide: use `image`.
- Pictures mixed with figures: use `bento`.
- One product screen to look at closely: use `screen`.

## Common mistakes

- **Putting the pictures in the body.** The pictures belong in `pictures:`; the body is one sentence. A list in the body fails [`deck lint`](/review/lint/) with `slot-content`: "The body slot holds a list of 2 items; the gallery layout sets it as text."
- **Long captions with four pictures.** The four-picture variant cuts captions to 40 characters. A 45-character caption is a `schema` error: "pictures[0].caption: The gallery layout sets at most 40 characters here (four pictures), got 45."
- **A wrong path.** A `src` that names no file is a `media-missing` error that prints the path it looked for. Paths are relative to the slide file with `./` or `../`, or written from the deck folder (`assets/street.jpg`).
- **A fifth picture.** `pictures:` takes at most four. Split the set over two slides, or pick the four that make the point.
- **Text over the photo.** The layout never sets text over a picture; a caption always stands under its picture. For a line over a photo, use `image`.

## Related layouts

- [`split`](/reference/layouts/split/): one picture with a title and text beside it.
- [`image`](/reference/layouts/image/): one photo edge to edge, with optional text over a scrim.
- [`bento`](/reference/layouts/bento/): tiles of unequal weight that mix pictures with figures and text.
- [`screen`](/reference/layouts/screen/): one product screen shown whole in a drawn frame, with numbered callouts.

How image paths resolve and what `deck lint` checks about images is on [media](/write-slides/media/).
