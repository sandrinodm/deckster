The `voices` layout sets two or three short testimonials as equal columns under a title that states the one point they support. Each has a quote, a name and a role, and optionally a photo. Use it when several customers or stakeholders describe the same experience in their own words.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
The `voices` layout needs `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. In 0.5.0, `deckster lint` reports `layout: There is no "voices" layout.`
</Aside>

Each entry in `voices:` is one column. The quote is set in the brand's quote style with a hanging quotation mark, and the name and role sit below on the same line across columns. `photo` adds a portrait beside the attribution, and `focal` keeps the face in view when it is cropped; the first photo here sets `focal: center 30%`. The `<Footnote>` gives the source and date of the interviews.

<SlideShot deck="lookbook" slide="voices-network" alt="Three short quotations in light type on a deep green slide, side by side under the title, each with a hanging quotation mark and followed by the speaker's name and role: a bakery owner, a pathology lead and a rider. None has a portrait." />

Three voices take shorter quotes and names, and the type is smaller than with two. Without a `photo` the attribution is the name and role alone, as here. This slide also sets `surface: inverse` and `reveal: true`.

## When to use it

- **Two or three speakers.** One speaker belongs on [`quote`](/reference/layouts/quote/). Each entry needs `quote`, `name` and `role`.
- **Short quotes.** A `quote` takes up to 100 characters, or 64 with three voices, and is set in at most three lines. A `name` takes up to 28 characters, or 20 with three voices, and a `role` up to 40. The title takes up to 60 characters, the body one sentence of up to 14 words and the footnote up to 16.
- **Write the words without quotation marks.** The layout sets them. Put the source and date in the `<Footnote>`, and say there if the testimonials are illustrative.
- **One point under the title.** The title is the claim, and the voices are the evidence for it. The body is one sentence of context; the testimonials never go in it.
- **Bring the speakers in one at a time** with `reveal: true`, and put a `[click]` cue for each in the notes. See [builds](/write-slides/builds/).
- **Treatments.** `treatment` applies to every portrait on the slide, and `treatment: plain` shows photos as they are.

## Common mistakes

- **Bullets in the body.** The testimonials belong in `voices:`, and the body takes text only. `deckster lint` reports a list with the `slot-content` rule:

  ```console
  $ deckster lint launch-review
  decks/launch-review/slides/v-list.mdx
    error   slot-content (v-list): The body slot holds a list of 2 items; the voices layout sets it as text.
            Fix: Write it as a sentence, or move the points to a layout made for a list (content or split; `deckster layouts`).

  launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings in 1 slide (1 step checked).
  ```

- **A long quote in a row of three.** Adding a third voice tightens the limits for all of them: `voices[0].quote: The voices layout sets at most 64 characters here (three voices), got 72.`
- **A single voice.** The schema reports `voices: Expected at least 2 voices, got a list of 1 item.` Use `quote` for one person.
- **Expecting an avatar without a photo.** Without `photo`, a speaker has no portrait or initials tile; the layout shows the name and role only. [`persona`](/reference/layouts/persona/) and [`bio`](/reference/layouts/bio/) draw an initials tile instead.
