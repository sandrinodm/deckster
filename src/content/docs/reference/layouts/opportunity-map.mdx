The west bank’s shops form a short loop from Quayside. Across the river, bridge queues could break the delivery promise. The route and crossing explain why one district comes first.

## Author the slide

Use a 16:9 base map. Put the district labels, proposed location, route points and constraint on the same coordinate system, measured in percentages from the top left. With `reveal: true`, the map opens with the districts. The first advance adds the proposed site and route, and the next adds the constraint.
