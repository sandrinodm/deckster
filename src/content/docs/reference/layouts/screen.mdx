Use the `screen` layout to show one product screen as large as the slide allows: an app, a dashboard or a page the audience should look at. The kit draws the frame around it from the brand (a browser window, a phone, a laptop or none), never crops the picture, and sets up to four numbered callouts beside it.

## Example

<SlideExample deck="lookbook" slide="screen-rider" />

`frame: phone` draws the device, so the file holds only the app. Each callout puts a numbered marker on the screen and its label beside the frame under the same number, never over the interface, and with `reveal: true` they arrive one per step. The slide sits on the gradient surface, the layout's default. The reference below shows `frame: browser` with an `address`.

## When to use it

- **The audience should look at a screen.** `src` and `alt` are required: a screenshot cut to the app itself, without its own browser bar or phone bezel, and up to 120 characters on what it shows.
- **You pick the frame.** `frame` is `browser` (the default), `phone`, `laptop` or `none`. Only `browser` shows an `address`, up to 40 characters; keep it to the host and a word, since a long one is cut with an ellipsis when the window is narrow.
- **You point at up to four parts.** Each callout has `x` and `y` (percent of the picture from its top-left corner) and a `label` of up to 32 characters. Put the point just beside or at the end of what it names, since the marker covers what is under it.

The title takes up to 60 characters. The body is one optional sentence of up to 14 words beside the screen, for when the title does not say what the audience is looking at. For two to four screens, use `gallery`; when the text matters as much as the picture, `split`; for a photo, `image`.

## Common mistakes

**Fields that need another field.** An `address` needs the browser frame, and `reveal` needs callouts to reveal. Both are checked when the slide is built:

```console
$ deck lint launch-review
decks/launch-review/slides/map.mdx
  error   schema (map): address: Only the browser frame shows an address; this screen's frame is phone.
          Fix: Write `frame: browser` to show the address, or leave `address` out.
  error   schema (map): reveal: `reveal` brings in the callouts one per step, and this screen has none.
          Fix: Add `callouts:` to point at parts of the screen, or leave `reveal` out.

launch-review: 2 errors, 0 warnings.
```

A `schema` finding stops lint before the slides are rendered ([Lint](/review/lint/) explains the passes).

**The tour in the body.** Points on the screen belong in `callouts:`, not in the body. A body of 20 words is a `content-budget` error: `The body slot holds 20 words; its layout allows 14.` A fifth point is a second slide.

**The device in the file.** A screenshot that still has its browser bar or phone bezel gets a second one from the frame. Crop it to the app, or write `frame: none`.
