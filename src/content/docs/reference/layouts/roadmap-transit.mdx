Network engineering and hub operations can work in parallel until the hub needs proof of returned parcels. The interchange marks the handoff that makes the July launch possible.

## Author the slide

Give the three `tracks` the same four `periods`. Use a stop label for an event and `null` for an empty stop. Set `transfer.stop` to 2 or 3 and `from` and `to` to the two track positions counted from one. Name what the receiving team needs in the transfer label and the launch condition in `milestone`. With `reveal: true`, show the tracks first, then the transfer, then the milestone.
