Use this page to choose a layout for a slide, or to check what a layout takes before you fill it. Every slide names one in its frontmatter (`layout: stats`); a slide that names none uses `default`. The index below shows each of the 32 layouts as it renders; the sections after it give the full entry. For how layouts and slots fit together, read [layouts and slots](/concepts/layouts-and-slots/); for how to fill them in a slide file, [MDX slides](/write-slides/mdx-slides/).

<LayoutIndex />

## How to read an entry

- **Frontmatter, surface, title.** The line to write, the surface the slide gets unless its frontmatter sets `surface`, and whether `title:` is required and how many characters it may have. "Optional" with no limit means the title names the slide in the outline and may not be shown.
- **PowerPoint.** The PowerPoint slide layout `deck export --pptx` maps the slide to, so its title and body stay editable placeholders. A layout without this line exports its text as free text boxes.
- **Description.** What the layout is for and how it sets its content.
- **Slots.** Each named region: the tag you write it with (`body` is the content outside any slot tag), what it accepts (text, bullets, an image, a component), and its budget in words, bullets or code lines. A `*` or a `required` mark means `deck lint` reports a `schema` error when the slot is empty. The budgets are the same in every brand; `deck lint` holds each slide to them.
- **Fields.** The layout's own frontmatter keys, with their default and allowed values. The keys every slide takes are on [slide frontmatter](/reference/slide-frontmatter/).
- **Variants.** A variant applies when the slide matches its condition (a field set, a slot filled, a number of items), and tightens the budget it lists.
- **Example.** A complete slide in that layout: `deck new slide <deck> <id> --layout <name>` writes this example as the new slide.

The same data comes from the CLI for the layouts your workspace has. `deck layouts <deck> --summary` prints one compact entry per layout:

```console
$ deck layouts launch-review --summary
default     surface default   title any
            One column of content under an accent rule, centred on the slide: a heading with a lead paragraph, or a heading with a short list.
            slots: body* heading/text/bullets/image/component 80 words 6 bullets
title       surface gradient  title <= 36
            The cover of a deck: the deck title set large on the bottom line, a one-line subtitle, and who is presenting and when.
            slots: body text 14 words; <Media> image
...
```

The output above is cut after two layouts. `deck layouts <deck> --json` prints everything this page shows, for every layout.
