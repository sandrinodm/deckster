A brand can bring slide layouts of its own: the slides of an organisation's template, such as a cover with a mark in one corner or a photo that runs off the edge. They live in `brands/<name>/layouts.ts`, next to `brand.ts`. This page shows you how to find a brand's layouts, put one on a slide, what happens when the deck moves to a brand without it, and how to write `layouts.ts` for a brand of your own so it passes `deck brand check`.

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
Brand layouts need `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 or later. 0.5.0 ignores `layouts.ts`, and a slide that names one of its layouts fails with `layout: There is no "fieldnote-section" layout.`
</Aside>

## The layouts a brand brings

Of the five published brands, `fieldnote` brings four layouts; `default`, `nocturne`, `kinetic` and `tidewater` bring none. Each falls back to a kit layout, which a deck on another brand renders instead.

| Layout | Label | Falls back to | Default surface |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fieldnote-cover` | Fieldnote cover | [`title`](/reference/layouts/title/) | `inverse` |
| `fieldnote-section` | Fieldnote section | [`section`](/reference/layouts/section/) | `accent` |
| `fieldnote-photo` | Fieldnote photo right | [`image`](/reference/layouts/image/) | `default` |
| `fieldnote-columns` | Fieldnote two columns | [`content`](/reference/layouts/content/) | `default` |

`deck brand <name>` names a brand's layouts on its `Layouts:` line (`Layouts: 4 (fieldnote-cover, fieldnote-section, fieldnote-photo, fieldnote-columns)`), and `deck layouts` lists them with the kit's. With `--summary`, the brand's layouts come first, under a heading that names the brand, each with the layout it falls back to. `--brand <name>` lists another brand's layouts than the deck's:

```console
$ deck layouts launch-review --brand fieldnote --summary
Layouts from the fieldnote brand (prefer these):
fieldnote-cover    surface inverse  title <= 36
                   A cover with a notebook margin rule down the left and a sprig mark top right: eyebrow, title, subtitle, byline and date set against the rule.
                   falls back to: title
                   slots: body text 14 words
fieldnote-section  surface accent   title <= 40
                   A part divider on terracotta: the part number large at the top left, the title low on the left over survey contour lines, and a one-line summary under a rule.
                   falls back to: section
                   slots: body text 16 words
fieldnote-photo    surface default  title <= 44
                   Text on the left under a double rule and a picture that runs off the right and bottom edges: eyebrow, title, a paragraph and a caption.
                   falls back to: image
                   slots: body text 24 words; <Caption> text 14 words; <Media>* image
fieldnote-columns  surface default  title <= 90
                   A title under an eyebrow with a rule below it, then two columns split by a hairline: the main text on the left and an aside, a note or a figure, on the right.
                   falls back to: content
                   slots: body* text/bullets/table/chart/component 72 words 6 bullets; <Aside> text/number 36 words

Other layouts:
default       surface default   title any
...
```

The output above is cut after the first kit layout. With `--json`, each layout also has `source` (`brand:fieldnote` for these, the package for the kit's), `fallback` and `label`.

## Put one on a slide

These steps use `fieldnote` in a workspace called `talks` with a deck called `launch-review`. To add the brand, see [use a brand](/brands/use-a-brand/).

<Steps>

1. Set the deck's brand in `decks/launch-review/deck.config.ts`. A deck draws a brand layout only in the brand that defines it.

   ```ts
   import { defineDeck } from '@deckster/engine/config';

   export default defineDeck({
     title: 'Launch review',
     brand: 'fieldnote',
     slides: ['title'],
   });
   ```

2. Add a slide in the layout. `deck new slide` takes a brand layout like any other:

   ```console
   $ deck new slide launch-review part-one --layout fieldnote-section
   decks/launch-review/slides/part-one.mdx
   decks/launch-review/deck.config.ts
   ```

   The slide is the fallback's example, cut down to what the brand layout shows:

   ```mdx
   ---
   layout: fieldnote-section
   title: Part one
   number: "02"
   ---

   Where we plant, in what order, and what it costs each year.

   <Notes>
   Say how long this part takes and what the committee will decide at its end.
   </Notes>
   ```

3. Lint the deck:

   ```console
   $ deck lint launch-review
   launch-review: no problems in 2 slides (2 steps checked).
   ```

</Steps>

A brand layout takes the slots and fields its boxes show, with the fallback's descriptions, limits and checks, so `fieldnote-section` takes `number` and the body but not `eyebrow` or `numeral`, which `section` also has. Its budgets are the fallback's or tighter. A slide's `surface` overrides the layout's default surface as on any layout.

Each box is fixed: the text steps down until it fits its box, but a box holds what it holds. A kit layout sets its content in the brand's margins and grid instead, and adapts to more or less of it. Use the brand's layouts for the slides its template has, and kit layouts for the rest.

## In another brand: the fallback

A deck can be rendered in another brand without an edit (see [use a brand](/brands/use-a-brand/#render-a-deck-in-another-brand)). A slide on a layout the other brand lacks renders with the layout's fallback, and `deck lint` reports `brand-layout-fallback`, a warning, for each such slide:

```console
$ deck lint launch-review --brand default
decks/launch-review/slides/part-one.mdx
  warning brand-layout-fallback (part-one): The slide names the layout "fieldnote-section" of the "fieldnote" brand, which this deck's brand does not have, so it renders with the section layout.
          Fix: Switch the deck back to that brand, or pick a layout from `deck layouts launch-review` and set it in the slide's frontmatter.

launch-review: 0 errors, 1 warning in 2 slides (2 steps checked).
```

The swap happens when the deck is built, from the `layouts.ts` of the other brands in the workspace's `brands/` folder, which are read as data and never run. The built deck names only the kit layout. A slide is valid on its fallback whenever it is valid on the brand layout, because the brand layout's contract is cut from the fallback's. A layout that no brand in the workspace defines is an error, as for any unknown layout: `layout: There is no "acme-cover" layout.` When two brands define the same layout name with different fallbacks, the build fails and asks you to rename one.

## Write layouts.ts

`layouts.ts` default-exports one `defineBrandLayouts` call around a list of plain values, like `brand.ts`: no imports other than `@deckster/brand`, no variables or functions. This is `fieldnote-section` from the published [`fieldnote` brand](https://github.com/sandrinodm/deckster/blob/main/brands/fieldnote/layouts.ts):

```ts
import { defineBrandLayouts } from '@deckster/brand';

export default defineBrandLayouts([
  {
    name: 'fieldnote-section',
    label: 'Fieldnote section',
    description:
      'A part divider on terracotta: the part number large at the top left, the title low on the left over survey contour lines, and a one-line summary under a rule.',
    fallback: 'section',
    surface: 'accent',
    title: { box: [144, 400, 1100, 400], type: 'headline', anchor: 'end' },
    regions: [
      { bind: { field: 'number' }, box: [144, 130, 600, 200], type: 'stat', color: 'accent' },
      { bind: { slot: 'body' }, box: [144, 876, 1040, 120], type: 'lead' },
    ],
    background: [{ type: 'image', file: 'layouts/section-contours.svg', fit: 'cover' }],
    layers: 'over',
  },
]);
```

Every box is `[x, y, width, height]` in pixels on the 1920x1080 canvas, measured from its top-left corner, not inside the brand's margins. A file holds up to 64 layouts. Each layout has:

| Field | Takes |
| --- | --- |
| `name` | Required. Kebab-case and unique in the file. It cannot be the name of a kit or engine layout: prefix it with the brand, as in `fieldnote-section`. |
| `label` | The layout's name in the source template. A PowerPoint export names the slide layout after it. |
| `description` | Required. When to use it; `deck layouts` prints it. |
| `fallback` | Required. The kit or engine layout a deck on another brand renders the slide with. It also sets which slots and fields the layout may take. |
| `surface` | The surface a slide on the layout gets unless its frontmatter sets one; without it, the fallback's. |
| `title` | Required. `{ box, type?, align?, anchor?, color? }`, or `false` for no title, which a fallback that requires one refuses. `type` is a type step (`title` by default), `color` a role, `align` `start`, `center` or `end` across the box (`start` by default), and `anchor` the same down it (`end` by default). |
| `regions` | Required, up to 12 boxes. `bind: { slot: 'body' }` shows a slot of the fallback, `bind: { field: 'eyebrow' }` one of its frontmatter fields as text. `kind` is `text` (the default), `media` for a picture, or `content`. A text region takes `type` (`body` for a slot and `label` for a field by default), `align`, `anchor` (`start` by default) and `color`; a media region takes `fit` (`cover`, the default, or `contain`) and `treatment`, one of the brand's image treatments. |
| `fields` | Fields of the fallback the layout accepts but does not draw, such as a setting. |
| `background` | The layout's art: one list of background layers for every surface, or one list per surface name. A surface without an entry shows its own layers. |
| `layers` | `replace` (the default) draws the art in place of the surface's background layers; `over` draws it on top of them. |
| `chrome` | `logo`, `footer`, `progress` and `pageNumber` set to `false` to hide them on the layout; `pageNumber` also takes a box to move the page number into. |
| `budget` | Per slot, `maxWords`, `maxBullets` and `maxLines`. Each limit is the lower of this one and the fallback's, so a budget can only tighten. |
| `source` | Where the layout came from: `file`, and optionally `master`, `layout` and `placeholders`. The `brand-import` skill writes it; the build does not read it. |

The art is made of the same background layers as a surface's `background` (`image`, `placed`, `pattern` and `gradient`; see [fonts and assets](/brands/fonts-and-assets/)), and its files sit in the brand folder: fieldnote keeps its six drawings in `brands/fieldnote/layouts/`. A media region that reaches two edges meeting at a corner covers that corner, and the page chrome that corner holds moves to the corner across the slide at the same height.

## Check it

`deck brand check <name>` checks `layouts.ts` after `brand.ts` and names it on its own line when the brand has one. Each problem has its path in the file and a fix. Here a layout takes a kit layout's name, and another binds a slot its fallback does not have:

```console
$ deck brand check fieldnote
brands/fieldnote/brand.ts
brands/fieldnote/layouts.ts
  error   layouts[2].name: The layout name "quote" is already a layout of the kit or the engine.
          Fix: Rename it, for example "<brand>-quote"; brand layouts never replace kit layouts.
  error   layouts[3].regions[2].bind: The fallback "content" has no slot "media".
          Fix: Bind one of its slots: body, aside, footnote.
fieldnote: cannot be used (2 errors, 0 warnings).
```

The other errors: a fallback that is not a kit or engine layout; a field in `fields` the fallback lacks; a slot or field the fallback requires that the layout does not take; a field bound to a text region that is not a string or a number; no title when the fallback requires one; a budget for a slot no region binds, or `maxLines` for a slot of prose; two layouts with one name; a slot or field bound twice; a box outside the canvas or smaller than 40x40 pixels; a surface, type step, role, treatment or gradient the brand does not have; and a layout with no title and no regions. Text boxes that overlap are a warning, and so is a media region on a slot that takes no pictures, or a text region on one that takes only pictures. A brand with errors in `layouts.ts` cannot be used, like one with errors in `brand.ts`; [check a brand](/brands/check-a-brand/) covers the rest of the check.

## In PowerPoint

`deck export --pptx` writes each brand layout the deck uses as a PowerPoint slide layout of its own, named after its `label` (`Fieldnote section`), with the art placed once in the slide layout and media regions as picture placeholders, so a slide's text stays editable on the template's own layout. The dry run counts the art:

```console
$ deck export launch-review --pptx --dry-run
PPTX dry run (hybrid mode): native / picture / simplified / dropped
01 title [title]: 4 / 0 / 0 / 0
02 part-one [fieldnote-section, art in layout: 1]: 6 / 0 / 0 / 0
Total: 10 native, 0 picture, 0 simplified, 0 dropped.
```

The rest of the export is on [PowerPoint](/export-and-publish/powerpoint/).

## From a template

The `brand-import` skill reads a PowerPoint template (`.potx`) or a deck's own slide layouts and writes `layouts.ts` with the art, a fallback for each layout and the boxes of its placeholders, for you to review against renders of the source. See [import a brand](/brands/import-a-brand/).
