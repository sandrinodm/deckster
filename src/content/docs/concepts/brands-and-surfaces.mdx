A slide's colours, fonts, type sizes, spacing and logo all come from its brand, and no slide file names a colour or a font. After reading this page you will know what a brand holds, how surfaces vary it from slide to slide, how a deck gets its brand, and why a brand that breaks the rules does not load.

## A brand is a typed file

A brand is a folder under `brands/` with a `brand.ts` file. The file is data, typed and checked, and it covers:

- **colour:** palettes (named scales of steps such as `cobalt.600`), the semantic roles built from them (`bg`, `surface`, `fg`, `heading`, `muted`, `accent`, `accent-fg`, `border`, `positive`, `negative`), the surfaces, and the data colours charts use;
- **gradients**, **typography** (a display, body and mono family and a scale of eleven type steps in canvas pixels), **shape** (radii, strokes, shadows) and **spacing** (a scale, slide margins and a grid);
- the **logo**, the **icon** set, **imagery** treatments, **chrome** (page number, footer, progress, logo placement) and **motion** (easings, durations, the slide transition).

The `default` brand is the only one that ships with the npm CLI. To use another, see [use a brand](/brands/use-a-brand/). Every field is listed in the [brand schema](/reference/brand-schema/).

## Surfaces

A surface is a named set of roles. A slide asks for `text-muted` or `bg-surface` and gets the colour that role has on its surface. Every brand has `default`, `inverse`, `accent` and `gradient` surfaces and can add more.

The four slides of the `hello` deck use one surface each. The site renders them in Fieldnote, whose `default` surface has a cream background and whose `inverse` has a deep green background.

Each [layout](/concepts/layouts-and-slots/) has a default surface: `title` and `closing` are on `gradient`, `section` on `accent`, `statement` and `image` on `inverse`, most others on `default`. A slide picks another with `surface:` in its frontmatter, and a surface the brand doesn't have fails the build:

```console
$ deckster build launch-review
transforming...
✓ 852 modules transformed.
✗ Build failed in 641ms
Invalid frontmatter in decks/launch-review/slides/why-now.mdx:
  - surface: The default brand has no "dark" surface.
    Fix: Use one of the default brand's surfaces: default, inverse, accent, gradient.
```

Surfaces give a deck its rhythm without any slide naming a colour. A surface can also paint background layers, such as a photo, a pattern or a gradient: see [palettes, surfaces and typography](/brands/palettes-surfaces-and-typography/).

## How a deck gets its brand

The deck's `deck.config.ts` names it with `brand: 'default'`, and a deck without that line uses `default`. To see a deck in another brand without editing anything, pass `--brand <name>` to `deckster dev`, `deckster present`, `deckster shot`, `deckster diff`, `deckster lint` or `deckster export`. The slide files stay the same: they name only roles, surfaces and type steps, which every brand defines.

## A brand that is wrong does not load

Before a brand is used, deckster checks it: the fields and their types, every palette step, gradient, surface and logo it refers to, and its colours. On every surface, text needs a WCAG contrast of 4.5:1 against its background, and graphics 3:1. The data colours must stay distinguishable on the chart background and under colour-vision deficiencies. A brand that fails stops `deckster build`, `deckster shot` and `deckster lint` before they render anything, and each error names the file, the field and the fix. A brand whose `muted` role was set to the pale `ink.200`:

```console
$ deckster brand check acme
brands/acme/brand.ts
  error   color.roles.muted: On the default surface, muted #d9dce4 on bg #fcfcfd has contrast 1.34:1; secondary text needs 4.5:1.
          Fix: Change muted to a color such as "#71747b", or change color.roles.bg.
  error   color.roles.muted: On the default surface, muted #d9dce4 on surface #f4f5f8 has contrast 1.26:1; secondary text on cards needs 4.5:1.
          Fix: Change muted to a color such as "#6d7077", or change color.roles.surface.
acme: cannot be used (2 errors, 0 warnings).
```

## Why slides never name a colour

The Tailwind theme a slide's classes come from holds only the brand's names. The colour, font, type size, radius, shadow and easing scales are replaced outright, so `text-red-500` or `font-serif` matches nothing. You write role classes such as `text-fg`, `text-muted` or `bg-surface`, and type steps such as `type-headline`, instead. A colour written by hand, for example in a `style` attribute, still renders. `deckster lint` measures the rendered slide and reports it as `off-brand`, as it does Tailwind numeric spacing and fonts or sizes outside the brand:

```console
$ deckster lint launch-review
decks/launch-review/slides/why-now.mdx
  error   off-brand (why-now step 0): "Support is fully staffed from February" uses the text color #e11d48, which is not a default brand token on the "default" surface.
          Fix: Use a role class such as `text-fg`, `text-muted`, `bg-surface` or `border-accent`, a palette step or a chart color instead of a hard-coded color. `deckster brand --json` lists every color.

launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings in 2 slides (2 steps checked).
```

[Lint rules](/reference/lint-rules/#colour) covers `off-brand` and `contrast`.
