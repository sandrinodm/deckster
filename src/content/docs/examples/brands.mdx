The same three slides of the [showcase](/examples/showcase/) deck in each of the five published brands. Use it to pick a look, and to see how much a brand changes: colours, type, icons, corners, backgrounds and chrome all move, and the slides stay the same.

**Nothing in the deck changes between these pictures.** Each grid is the same slide file rendered with `deck shot showcase <slide> --brand <name>`; only the brand differs. That works because slides hold no colours, fonts or pixel positions: the layout places the content, and the brand supplies every value it draws with ([Brands and surfaces](/concepts/brands-and-surfaces/) explains the model).

## A diagram

`lint-path` is a `<Flow>` diagram on the `diagram` layout. Each brand has its own diagram look: the nodes' fills, outlines, corners, shadows and title type, the edges' bends and arrowheads, and the edge labels as plain text or chips. [Diagram look and cards](/brands/diagram-look-and-cards/) shows how a brand sets them.

## The five brands

| Brand | For | Display and body type | Icons | Accent on content slides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `default` | Every deck unless its config names another. Light-first: cool grays, one cobalt accent, a cobalt-to-ink gradient | Bricolage Grotesque over Inter, JetBrains Mono for code | Lucide | `#2d4ae0` cobalt |
| `nocturne` | Dark-first and editorial: violet-black backgrounds, a grainy aurora gradient, sharp corners, slow transitions | Fraunces over Manrope, IBM Plex Mono for labels and code | Tabler | `#ff8f6b` ember |
| `fieldnote` | Research findings, reports and talks that should feel considered: cream paper, forest green with terracotta and ochre | Playfair Display over Source Sans 3, Source Code Pro as the mono face | Tabler | `#2c5e44` forest green |
| `kinetic` | Product launches, developer talks and demo days: chalk and ink with electric blue and acid lime, square corners, hard offset shadows | Space Grotesk over Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono for labels and code | Tabler | `#3d4bff` electric blue |
| `tidewater` | Climate, health, public-sector and finance decks that carry numbers people act on: warm sand, deep sea blues, a coral accent used sparingly | Hanken Grotesk throughout, IBM Plex Mono as the mono face | Lucide | `#215f76` sea blue |

Every brand has the four surfaces `default`, `inverse`, `accent` and `gradient`. Three add one of their own that a slide picks with `surface:` in its frontmatter: `notebook` in `fieldnote` (ruled paper with a margin line), `grid` in `kinetic` (a dot grid) and `shore` in `tidewater` (faint contour lines). Their palettes, surfaces and type scales are on [Palettes, surfaces and typography](/brands/palettes-surfaces-and-typography/).

## Try one

Only `default` ships with the npm CLI. The other four are folders in the repository's `brands/`: `deck brand add` (new in 0.6.0) copies one into your workspace and names the font packages to install; [Use a brand](/brands/use-a-brand/) has the steps. To make a look of your own, see [Create a brand](/brands/create-a-brand/).
