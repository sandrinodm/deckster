The lookbook is one deck that shows everything the kit can make: every layout and every layout option, every chart, diagram and slide component, all four surfaces, all eight slide transitions, the five `<Step>` effects, `focus`, a count-up and a `<Morph>` pair. Use this page to find a slide that looks like the one you need and copy it into your own deck.

<DeckEmbed deck="lookbook" slide="cover" />

It tells one story, so it reads as a talk rather than a specimen sheet: "Same hour, no van", the launch-day talk of Wrenhaul, a cargo-bike delivery company. **Wrenhaul, its people, customers and every figure are invented.** The deck has 180 slides in 12 parts and 364 steps. Its slides' budgets add up to 122 minutes 35 seconds, and `deck.config.ts` sets `duration: '127m'`. The whole deck is at [/decks/lookbook/](/decks/lookbook/).

<Aside type="note" title="New in v0.6.0">
42 of the 180 slides use the 20 layouts that are new in `@deckster/cli` 0.6.0 (the [layouts reference](/reference/layouts/) lists them). In 0.5.0, `deck lint` reports `layout: There is no "<name>" layout.` for each of those slides, so the deck builds and lints only with 0.6.0 or later.
</Aside>

## Find what you need

- **Parts are named after the job a slide does.** Look in the part named after your problem: "Compare and decide", "Prove it with a number".
- **Slide ids say what the slide shows**, such as `split-photo-left-bleed`, `chart-waterfall` or `motion-wipe`. They are also the file names in `slides/`.
- **The first line of every slide's speaker notes names its recipe**: the layout and the options that make the slide, and when to use it. Open the presenter window, or read the `<Notes>` block in the source.

## Copy a slide

Here is a split slide with a picture that bleeds off the left edge. The source tab is the file to copy; the first line of its notes is the recipe:

<SlideExample deck="lookbook" slide="split-photo-left-bleed" />

<Steps>

1. Copy the slide file from the source tab, or from `decks/lookbook/slides/<id>.mdx` in the [repository](https://github.com/sandrinodm/deckster/tree/main/decks/lookbook), into your deck's `slides/` folder.

2. Copy the files the slide names. Pictures are in the lookbook's `assets/` folder (this one uses `../assets/scenes/handover.svg`), and their sources and licences are in `assets/CREDITS.txt`. A few chart slides read CSV files from `data/`, and the mini app and live widget slides import code from `lib/` and `apps/`. Keep the same relative paths, or replace the files with your own.

3. Add the slide id to `slides` in your `deck.config.ts`, rewrite the text for your talk, and run `deck lint <deck>`.

</Steps>

To run the whole lookbook in your workspace, copy `decks/lookbook/` from the repository and delete its `package.json` and `tsconfig.json`, as for the [showcase](/examples/showcase/). With 0.6.0 it lints clean (0.5.0 refuses 42 of the slides):

```console
$ deck lint lookbook
lookbook: no problems in 180 slides (364 steps checked).
```

## How it stays complete

A test in the repository, [`lookbook-coverage.test.ts`](https://github.com/sandrinodm/deckster/blob/main/packages/cli/src/lookbook/lookbook-coverage.test.ts), reads the kit's catalogs and the lookbook's slide files and fails with a list of what the deck does not show yet: layouts, layout option values, `reveal: true`, surfaces, transitions, step effects, `focus`, a `<Morph>` pair, charts, diagram components and slide components. A layout or component added to the kit fails that test until the lookbook has a slide for it.

Every layout's fields, slots and budgets are on its own page under [layouts](/reference/layouts/). The motion part pairs with [Builds](/write-slides/builds/) and [Transitions and morphs](/write-slides/transitions-and-morphs/).
