## Use one in your own deck

Copy the example’s MDX into your deck’s `slides/` folder, along with any referenced images or data at the same relative paths. Add its slide ID to `slides` in `deck.config.ts` and run `deck lint <deck>`. For a screenshot example, replace the screens with your own captures before adjusting the crop or annotations. [Lookbook copy instructions](/examples/lookbook/#copy-a-slide)

Try the live demo and inspect the finished slide. The version wipe exports both complete screens, and the branching demo exports both outcomes. The AI examples use authored responses and local interactions, so you can rehearse the same decision and inspect both review choices. [Builds](/write-slides/builds/)
