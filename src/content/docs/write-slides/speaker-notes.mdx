Write what the speaker says under each slide, cue every step, and give each slide a time budget so the presenter window can tell you whether the talk is on pace. This page is also the checklist for reviewing notes an agent wrote.

## Add notes to a slide

In an MDX slide, put a `<Notes>` block at the end. Its content is Markdown:

```mdx
---
layout: statement
title: Opening
budget: 45s
---

We ship on Thursday.

<Notes>
Say the date first, then stop. The rest of the talk is how we get there.
</Notes>
```

In a TSX slide, import `Notes` from `@deckster/engine` and render it inside the slide; the build reads its text without running the slide, so keep it to text. See [TSX slides](/write-slides/tsx-slides/).

## What belongs in them

Notes are what the speaker says and does, not a copy of the slide. Text the audience must read belongs on the slide.

- **What the slide proves**, in a sentence, and the point in spoken language.
- **A `[click]` cue for each step**, in order, where that part is spoken. A slide that builds in four steps has four cues.
- **Cues for anything else** the speaker does: pause, ask the room, switch to the demo.
- **Sources and caveats** for questions, after an "If asked" line.
- **The transition** into the next slide.

## Give a slide a time budget

`budget:` in the frontmatter is how long you plan to speak on the slide, in minutes and seconds: `30s`, `2m`, `1m30s`. Anything else fails the build:

```console
$ deckster lint launch-review
decks/launch-review/slides/plan.mdx
  error   schema (plan): budget: Expected a duration such as "90s", "2m" or "1m30s", got "30 seconds".
          Fix: Write the speaking time as minutes and seconds, for example `budget: 90s` or `1m30s`.

launch-review: 1 error, 0 warnings.
```

The talk's length is `duration` in `deck.config.ts` (`deckster new <deck> --duration 20m` writes it):

```ts
export default defineDeck({
  title: 'Launch review',
  brand: 'default',
  duration: '20m',
  slides: ['opening', 'plan'],
});
```

## What uses the budgets

**The presenter window** paces the talk against them, scaled to fit the `duration` when there is one, and leaves out hidden slides (new in 0.5.0). It needs at least one budget: a deck with a `duration` and no budgets shows No budgets, because the duration alone is not spread over the slides. [How pacing works](/present/presenter-tools-and-shortcuts/#how-pacing-works) explains the figures it shows.

**`deckster lint`** adds the budgets up. When they come to more than the deck's `duration`, `time-budget` is an error; without a `duration` the rule reports nothing. A [hidden slide](/write-slides/hidden-slides/) is not in the talk, so its budget is left out of the total (new in 0.5.0). A slide without notes is a `missing-notes` warning:

```console
$ deckster lint launch-review
decks/launch-review/slides/plan.mdx
  warning missing-notes (plan): The slide has no speaker notes.
          Fix: Add a `<Notes>` block saying what the slide proves and what to say, in a sentence or two.

decks/launch-review/deck.config.ts
  error   time-budget: The slides' budgets add up to 1:15; the talk has 1:00.
          Fix: Cut slides, shorten their budgets, or move detail into an appendix.

launch-review: 1 error, 1 warning in 2 slides (2 steps checked).
```

More on reading findings is on [lint](/review/lint/).

## Where notes show

- In the presenter window, beside the current and next slide.
- In the PDF, as a notes page (the slide with its notes) after the slides, with `deckster export <deck> --notes`. See [PDF](/export-and-publish/pdf/).
- In the PowerPoint export, as each slide's speaker notes.
